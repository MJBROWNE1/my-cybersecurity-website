Why Is Cybersecurity Important?

Cybersecurity is important because it keeps your personal information, money, and devices safe from bad people who try to steal, trick, or harm you online.

Think of it like locking your house at night — if you don’t, anyone can walk in. Without cybersecurity, hackers can break into your accounts, steal your identity, or mess with your computer.

We use the internet for everything: banking, shopping, texting, school, work, and more. That means our private information is out there — and it needs to be protected.

Without good cybersecurity:

  • Your accounts could get hacked
  • Your money could be stolen
  • Your photos and files could be lost
  • Your identity could be used for scams

With good cybersecurity:

  • You stay safe online
  • Your private info stays private
  • You avoid scams, viruses, and fake messages
  • You protect not just yourself, but your family and workplace too
