Common Cyber Threats & How to Avoid Them

1. Phishing

What it is: Fake emails or messages trying to trick you into clicking a bad link or giving out personal info.

How to prevent: Don’t click links from unknown senders, double-check email addresses, and never share personal info unless you're sure who you're talking to.

2. Malware

What it is: Harmful software that sneaks onto your device and can steal, delete, or lock your files.

How to prevent: Keep software updated, avoid sketchy downloads, and use antivirus software.

3. Ransomware

What it is: A type of malware that locks your files and demands money to unlock them.

How to prevent: Backup your files, avoid clicking unknown links, and use strong security software.

4. Password Attacks

What it is: Hackers try to guess or steal your passwords.

How to prevent: Use strong, unique passwords, enable two-factor authentication, and use a password manager.

5. Public Wi-Fi Attacks

What it is: Hackers spy on you when you're using public Wi-Fi, like at a coffee shop.

How to prevent: Avoid sensitive activities on public Wi-Fi, use a VPN, and disable auto-connect.

6. Social Engineering

What it is: Someone pretending to be someone you trust to trick you.

How to prevent: Always double-check identities, don't give out personal info unless you're sure, and watch for pressure tactics.

7. Spyware

What it is: Software that secretly watches what you do and shares that info with others.

How to prevent: Don’t install unknown apps, use antivirus, and regularly scan your device.

8. Data Breaches

What it is: When hackers break into a company and steal user data like your name, email, and passwords.

How to prevent: Use different passwords for different sites, change them after a breach, and monitor your accounts for strange activity.
