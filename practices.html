Everyday Cybersecurity Best Practices

1. Use Strong Passwords

Make passwords long (at least 12 characters), mix in letters, numbers, and symbols, and avoid using the same password for multiple accounts.

2. Think Before You Click

Don't click links from people you don't know. Be careful with pop-ups, ads, and messages that sound too good to be true.

3. Keep Your Software Updated

Always install updates for your apps, phone, and computer. Updates fix security bugs hackers can use.

4. Turn On Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

Add extra protection to your accounts by using 2FA — it usually sends a code to your phone when you log in.

5. Back Up Your Files

Save important stuff to the cloud or an external drive. If something happens to your device, your files are safe.

6. Be Careful on Public Wi-Fi

Don't do banking or shopping on public Wi-Fi. Use a VPN if you need to connect to open networks.

7. Watch Out for Phishing

Don't share private info over email or messages. Look for weird email addresses or messages that ask for personal details.

8. Download Apps from Trusted Sources

Only get apps from the official stores like Google Play or the App Store. Check reviews and app permissions.

9. Limit What You Share Online

Don't post too much about yourself online. Hackers can use that info to trick you or guess your passwords.

10. Use Antivirus & Security Tools

Install antivirus programs on your devices and run regular scans to stay protected.
