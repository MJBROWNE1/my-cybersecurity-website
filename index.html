Welcome to Cybersecurity Awareness

As the internet becomes the center point of everything that we do in our everyday lives, protecting our information and computing systems is critical. This website will teach you the importance of cyber security, best practices, common threats, and various training and resource guides.

What You'll Learn:


Awareness – What cybersecurity means and why you should care

Importance – How it protects your money, identity, and personal info

Best Practices – Easy tips to help you stay safe every day

Threats to Avoid – Common online scams, viruses, and how to spot them

Resources – Trusted websites and tools to learn even more

Training – Free videos and lessons to build your knowledge.
